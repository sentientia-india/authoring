Trust center

Clear operating boundaries for customer courses and learner data.

These launch policies describe the service controls implemented by the platform. Contract-specific terms and regional addenda may supplement them.

PrivacyTermsRetentionSupportLimitsSubprocessors

01

Privacy

Customer source files, authored courses, learner identities, enrollments, attempts, and reporting events are processed only to provide the service. Tenant boundaries are enforced in application repositories. Secrets and raw private source content are excluded from application metrics and structured logs.

Customers control the lawful basis, notices, and consent required for information they upload or collect. Requests for access, export, correction, or deletion may be sent to privacy@samratcourse.com.

02

Service terms

Customers retain ownership of their source material and course output. They must have permission to upload content and must review generated material before publication. The service may not be used for unlawful material, credential theft, malware, deceptive assessment, or infringement.

Hosted availability targets are service objectives, not a guarantee unless a signed order states otherwise. Accounts may be limited or suspended to protect customers, the platform, or third parties from abuse or security risk.

03

Retention, export, and deletion

Active customer content is retained while an account is active. Operational backups expire through the documented recovery rotation. Before deletion, an authorized account owner can request a tenant export with integrity evidence. Deletion removes customer projects, releases, media, hosted learner content, and delivery objects.

Security audit, subscription, entitlement, usage, and billing evidence may be pseudonymized and retained where required for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. A legal hold overrides ordinary deletion until released.

04

Support and incidents

Support requests are accepted at support@samratcourse.com. Security reports go to security@samratcourse.com. Suspected cross-tenant access, data loss, billing corruption, or a critical vulnerability is treated as a stop-ship incident.

Target response: critical incidents within four hours, high-impact issues within one business day, and standard requests within two business days. Incident updates are posted on the public status page when customer impact is confirmed.

05

Service limits

Plans may limit exports, storage, generation jobs, hosted learners, event volume, and request rate. Uploads are restricted by size, file count, type, path safety, and decompression ratio. A single hosted package is currently limited to 60 MiB. Limits may be reduced temporarily to preserve service stability.

06

Subprocessors

The deployment may use an infrastructure host, PostgreSQL-compatible storage, S3-compatible object storage, Stripe for payments, a configured transactional-email provider, and customer-selected AI providers. The exact production list, regions, and transfer terms must be published before accepting personal data in a live commercial account.
